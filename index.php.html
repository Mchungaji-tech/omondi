The Shepherd

Born in the tea hills of Kipkelion and saved at an open-air crusade in Nakuru, Rev. Samuel Langat has spent twenty-three years planting churches, training pastors and raising the young along the Rift Valley. Beacon Gospel Centre, which he founded under a canvas tent in 2008, now gathers over two thousand souls on the hill at Langas.

He preaches one thing only — Christ, and Him crucified — with an open Bible, a warm heart, and the conviction that no one is beyond the reach of grace.

— Rev. Samuel K. Langat

(01) — The Journey

From the
Tea Hills

Every step held by a faithful God — this is the road the Master walked me down, from Kipkelion to the hill at Langas.

1981

Born in Kipkelion

Raised by my grandparents among the tea estates of Kericho. A home of little means but much prayer — my grandmother's worn Bible shaped my ear for the Word.

Roots
1999

Salvation at a Crusade

Met Christ at an open-air crusade in Nakuru town. Walked forward on a dusty field and have never looked back. Baptised that same month in the River Njoro.

Salvation
2003

School of Theology

Diploma in Pastoral Ministry. While studying, opened a house fellowship in Langas with nine members — eight neighbours and one very stubborn goat tied outside.

Training
2008

Beacon Gospel Centre Planted

Ordained into full-time ministry. Planted the church in Langas, Eldoret — forty souls under a canvas tent, rain or shine.

Church Plant
2013

Radio Ministry Begins

“Morning with God” launched on Voice of the Gospel 94.6 FM Eldoret — every Wednesday at 6:00 a.m. Listeners write from as far as Lodwar and Lamu.

Airwaves
2016

Beacon Youth Mission Founded

First youth camp at Soy Hills with 120 teenagers. Today it draws over 600 young people every August — worship, football, and riverside baptisms.

Youth
2021

New Sanctuary Dedicated

By the giving of widows and businessmen alike, a 1,500-seat auditorium was raised on the Langas hill. The congregation now stands above 2,000 members.

Sanctuary
2025

Jubilee Conference

Hosted pastors from 14 counties for the 25-years-of-grace conference in Eldoret. Twelve new church plants commissioned across the Rift Valley.

Jubilee

(04) — The Work

Our Ministries

01

Youth MinistryAges 13–30

Discipleship cells, football leagues, worship nights and career mentorship. Raising a generation that knows the Book and their calling.

✦ 400+ youth · 18 cell groups · annual Soy Hills camp
02

Women's FellowshipMothers' Union

Prayer bands, table-banking savings groups and mentorship for mothers — spiritual fire with financial dignity.

✦ 26 savings groups · 340 women · monthly prayer mountain
03

Children's ChurchAges 3–12

Sunday school in three languages, memory-verse clubs and the Huruma feeding programme — hot meals and the Bread of Life.

✦ 500+ children · 300 meals served weekly
04

Street Rescue OutreachRescue & Rehab

Rescue, rehabilitation and reconciliation for street-connected young people in Langas and Huruma — back to family, school and church.

✦ 210 youth resettled since 2018 · 3 partner churches
05

Beacon Leadership SchoolPastoral Training

A two-year evening school equipping bi-vocational pastors and lay leaders across the North Rift — hermeneutics, homiletics and shepherding.

✦ 60 pastors trained yearly · 14 counties reached
